How this works
  • Each team's build is Claude's read of its roster: categories where it ranks 10th or worse are treated as punts. The real managers' plans may differ.
  • Projections blend this season's stats with ESPN projections. Win chances come from simulating each week thousands of times.
  • Pickups must play 21+ minutes this season; free agents are preferred over waiver claims. Each team gets 4 adds a week.
  • Locks are at each day's first tip-off, usually about 8 AM Malaysia time.
  • Sources: ESPN league data, Locked On Fantasy Basketball. Prepared by Claude; numbers are a snapshot from the time shown at the top.
